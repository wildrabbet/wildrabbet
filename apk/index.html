Built for speed

A remote desktop without the heavy interface.

The companion stays small. Your PC does the capture and encoding; the phone focuses on decoding, display, and controls.

01Adjustable picture

Adaptive, 1080p, native-resolution, and game presets. Choose 30, 45, or 60 fps and set H.264 bandwidth yourself.

02Wi-Fi or USB

Use your private local network for convenience, or route the stream through a cable for a steadier low-latency connection.

03Complete PC control

Direct touch, trackpad mode, left/right/middle click, drag, two-finger scroll, phone keyboard, modifiers, and clipboard transfer.

04Custom gamepad

Use a virtual Xbox controller with remappable buttons, adjustable sticks, deadzones, sensitivity, opacity, scale, and haptics.

05Launch your games

Browse installed Steam games and Rabbit Kit shortcuts from the phone, launch one, and move straight into fullscreen control.

06Phone to PC too

Rabbit Kit can mirror and control a connected Android phone in a resizable window or fullscreen, including phone audio.

Three steps

Connect without an account.

Download and openTap Download above, open the file, and approve Android's normal install screen.
Enable Phone RemoteOn the PC, open Rabbit Kit → Cloud → Control this PC from your phone.
Pair onceChoose the PC in Rabbit Remote and enter the six-digit code shown by Rabbit Kit.
Private-network design. The remote picture, input, and clipboard stay between your paired devices on the same trusted Wi-Fi or USB cable. The LAN connection is not internet-encrypted, so do not forward its ports or use it on an untrusted public network. The app contacts rabbet.lol only to check whether an update is available.
